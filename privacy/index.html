Privacy Policy

The Cipher (“we”) is an end-to-end encrypted messenger. This policy explains the little data we handle and why.

What we can’t see

Your messages, images, notes hidden in images, and call audio and video are encrypted on your device before they leave it. We don’t have the keys, so we can’t read or hear them, and we can’t hand them over.

Phone numbers are checked with our SMS provider (Twilio) to confirm they are real mobile numbers and to send the code. Calls are relayed by LiveKit and are encrypted in transit. Data is stored with Supabase.

What we never do

No address-book upload. No ads, no tracking, no selling data, no third-party analytics.

Your choices

Delete your account at any time in Veil › Delete account. That permanently erases your account, pairings and messages from our servers. Burn timers, capture alerts and the disguise are all controlled by you.

Children

The Cipher is not for children under 13.

Law enforcement

We can only provide the limited account data listed above, and only when valid legal process requires it. We can’t provide message content because we don’t have it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
