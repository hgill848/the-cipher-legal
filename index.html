Privacy Policy Support

The Cipher is an iOS app. Your messages, images and calls are encrypted on your iPhone before they leave it. We don’t have the keys, so we can’t read or hear them.

Accounts are created with Sign in with Apple, Google, or a mobile number, and every account confirms a real mobile number. You only talk to people who share an invite with you.

Questions or account help: support@thecrownslegacy.in.
