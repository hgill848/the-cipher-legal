Your account

  • Delete your account: Veil › Delete account
  • “Use a real mobile number”: virtual, VoIP, toll-free and temporary numbers aren’t accepted. This keeps every account a real person.
  • “This iPhone already has an account”: each iPhone can hold one account. Delete the old account on that phone first.
  • Lost the unlock secret? Veil › Reset unlock secret (while unlocked). If you’re locked out, reinstall the app and sign in again. Your encrypted history can’t be recovered without the device key.
